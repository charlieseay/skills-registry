# Cheatsheet — The Coaching Habit

## The Seven Essential Questions, in order

1. **"What's on your mind?"** — open
2. **"And what else?"** — deepen (ask 3-5x, until "there is nothing else")
3. **"What's the real challenge here for you?"** — focus
4. **"What do you want?"** — surface the want/need
5. **"How can I help?"** — force a clear request, don't guess
6. **"If you're saying Yes to this, what are you saying No to?"** — protect capacity
7. **"What was most useful for you?"** — close (Coaching Bookends with #1)

## The fast 1-2-3 chain

```
Open:    "What's on your mind?"
Check:   "Is there anything else on your mind?"
Focus:   "So what's the real challenge here for you?"
Deepen:  "And what else [is the real challenge here for you]?"
Probe:   "Is there anything else?"
Land:    "So... what's the real challenge here for you?"
```

## New Habit Formula (for building any of these into a habit)

```
WHEN THIS HAPPENS...  <specific trigger: person, moment, feeling>
INSTEAD OF...          <the old habit, stated precisely>
I WILL...              <the new behavior, <60 seconds to do>
```

## Softening phrases (use when a question could land as blunt)

- "Out of curiosity..."
- "Just so I know..."
- "To help me understand better..."
- "To make sure that I'm clear..."

## Ground rules

- **One question at a time.** Ask, then stop.
- **Actually listen** to the answer — not performative nodding.
- **Get comfortable with silence** — wait 3-4 seconds before re-filling the space.
- **"What," not "Why."** Why puts people on the defensive and pulls toward fixing.
- **There's still a place for advice** — the goal is a better default ratio, not zero advice ever.

## Quick diagnostic: which question do I need right now?

| Situation | Question |
|---|---|
| Conversation hasn't started / small talk | Kickstart |
| Their first answer is probably not their best/only one | AWE |
| They've named 5 problems, or someone else's problem, or stayed abstract | Focus |
| Unclear what they're actually asking for | Foundation or Lazy |
| "How do I...?" and you want to answer | Lazy → AWE on their ideas |
| Someone's about to overcommit | Strategic |
| Conversation/meeting is ending | Learning |
