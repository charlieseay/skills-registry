# Glossary of Financial Planning Terms

## A

**Annuity** - Investment contract guaranteeing income stream for life or specified period. Fixed annuity guarantees rate; variable annuity's return depends on underlying investments.

**Adjusted Gross Income (AGI)** - Total income minus certain allowable deductions. Used to determine tax liability and eligibility for various credits and benefits.

**Asset Allocation** - Division of investment portfolio among asset classes (stocks, bonds, cash) based on risk tolerance and time horizon.

**Asset Class** - Category of investments with similar characteristics (stocks, bonds, real estate, commodities).

**Automatic Stay** - Bankruptcy court order halting creditor collection activities immediately upon filing.

## B

**Beneficiary** - Person designated to receive proceeds from life insurance, retirement accounts, estate, or trust.

**Bond** - Debt security representing loan to corporation or government. Issuer pays periodic interest and repays principal at maturity.

**Buy-Sell Agreement** - Contract between business owners specifying procedure for transferring ownership if owner dies, becomes disabled, or leaves business.

## C

**Capital Gain** - Profit from sale of asset. Long-term (held >1 year) taxed at preferential rates; short-term taxed as ordinary income.

**Cash Flow** - Movement of money in and out. Net cash flow = income minus expenses.

**Charitable Remainder Trust** - Irrevocable trust providing income to donor for life, with remainder to charity.

**CFP Board** - Certified Financial Planner Board of Standards; certifies and regulates financial planning professionals.

**Community Property** - Property acquired during marriage owned equally by both spouses (nine community property states).

**Conservatorship** - Court-supervised arrangement where conservator manages incapacitated person's affairs.

**Coverdell Education Savings Account (ESA)** - Tax-advantaged education savings account ($2,000 annual limit, age 30 termination).

## D

**Disability Insurance** - Income protection insurance replacing portion of salary if unable to work due to illness/injury.

**Diversification** - Spreading investments across different assets to reduce risk.

**Dollar-Cost Averaging** - Investing fixed amount at regular intervals, reducing impact of volatility.

**Durable Power of Attorney** - Legal document surviving principal's incapacity, authorizing agent to act on principal's behalf.

## E

**Earned Income** - Income from employment or self-employment (wages, salary, business income). Subject to income and self-employment tax.

**EITC (Earned Income Tax Credit)** - Refundable tax credit for low-income working families.

**Engagement Letter** - Written agreement between advisor and client defining scope of services, fees, and responsibilities.

**ERISA** - Employee Retirement Income Security Act; federal law regulating qualified retirement plans.

**Estate Planning** - Process of organizing affairs and designating how property transfers at death and during incapacity.

**Estate Tax** - Federal tax on transfer of property at death (applies to estates exceeding $13.61 million in 2024).

**Executor** - Person designated to administer estate and carry out terms of will.

## F

**FAFSA** - Free Application for Federal Student Aid; determines federal financial aid eligibility.

**Fiduciary** - Person with legal duty to act in another's best interest (trustee, executor, investment advisor).

**Fiduciary Duty** - Legal obligation to act solely in client's interest, disclose conflicts, manage conflicts that cannot be avoided.

**Fixed Annuity** - Annuity guaranteeing fixed interest rate and income.

**401(k)** - Employer-sponsored retirement plan allowing employee pre-tax contributions with potential employer match.

**529 Plan** - Qualified tuition plan for education savings with tax-deferred growth and tax-free withdrawals for education.

**Financial Planning** - Comprehensive process of formulating, implementing, and monitoring coordinated financial decisions.

## G

**Gift Tax** - Federal tax on lifetime transfers of property ($18,000 annual exclusion per recipient in 2024; excess uses lifetime exemption).

**Grantor** - Person creating and funding trust.

**Guardian** - Person appointed to make decisions for minor child or incapacitated adult.

## H

**Healthcare Power of Attorney** - Document designating agent to make healthcare decisions if principal unable.

**HELOC** - Home Equity Line of Credit; revolving line of credit secured by home equity.

**Homestead Exemption** - Protection of primary residence from creditors (amount varies by state).

## I

**IRA (Individual Retirement Account)** - Tax-advantaged retirement savings account. Traditional IRA offers pre-tax contributions; Roth offers tax-free growth.

**Irrevocable Trust** - Trust that cannot be changed or revoked after creation. Removes assets from grantor's estate.

**Investment Advisor** - Professional providing investment advice; subject to fiduciary duty if registered.

## K

**Kiddie Tax** - Tax rule applying parent's tax rate to certain unearned income of dependent children.

## L

**Life Insurance** - Insurance providing lump sum payment to beneficiaries upon insured's death. Term insurance temporary; whole life permanent.

**Liquid Assets** - Assets readily convertible to cash (checking, savings, money market).

**Living Will** - Document expressing end-of-life care wishes if unable to communicate.

**Long-Term Capital Gain** - Investment profit on asset held >1 year, taxed at preferential 0%, 15%, or 20% rates.

## M

**Marital Deduction** - Unlimited deduction for property transfers to spouse, eliminating gift/estate tax on such transfers.

**Means Test** - Income and expense test determining Chapter 7 bankruptcy eligibility.

**Medicaid** - State-administered health insurance for low-income individuals.

**Medicare** - Federal health insurance program for individuals 65+ and some disabled individuals.

**Mentor** - Experienced professional providing guidance and support.

## N

**Net Worth** - Assets minus liabilities; measure of financial position.

**Non-Qualified Investment** - Investment account without tax-advantaged status; growth subject to annual income tax.

## O

**Ordinary Income** - Income taxed at ordinary rates (wages, interest, business income); does not include capital gains.

## P

**Pass-Through Entity** - Business entity (partnership, S-corp, LLC) where profits flow through to owner tax returns.

**Payable-on-Death (POD)** - Bank account allowing assets to transfer directly to named beneficiary without probate.

**Pension** - Employer-provided retirement benefit; defined benefit plan guarantees specific benefit.

**Pour-Over Will** - Will directing probate assets to revocable trust to coordinate with trust plan.

**Power of Attorney** - Legal document authorizing agent to act on principal's behalf (may be limited or general).

**Probate** - Court process validating will and transferring probate property; subject to delays, costs, and publicity.

## Q

**QDRO** - Qualified Domestic Relations Order; court order dividing retirement account upon divorce without penalties.

**Qualified Plan** - Retirement plan meeting IRS requirements for tax deductions and tax-deferred growth (401(k), IRA, etc.).

**QTIP Trust** - Qualified Terminable Interest Property trust; provides spouse income with remainder to other beneficiaries.

## R

**Roth IRA** - Individual retirement account with post-tax contributions, tax-free growth, and tax-free withdrawals.

**Revocable Living Trust** - Trust retaining grantor's control during life and transferring property at death without probate.

**Risk Tolerance** - Individual's comfort level with investment volatility based on financial capacity, psychological comfort, and attitude.

**Rollover** - Transfer of retirement account funds from one plan to another without triggering tax.

## S

**SEC** - Securities and Exchange Commission; federal regulator of securities markets and investment advisors.

**Self-Employment Tax** - Social Security and Medicare tax on self-employed income (15.3% of net earnings).

**Separate Property** - Property owned by one spouse before marriage or acquired by gift/inheritance (not divided in divorce).

**Series EE/I Bonds** - U.S. savings bonds with government guarantee, tax deferral, and education tax benefits.

**SEP-IRA** - Simplified Employee Pension IRA; allows high contributions (up to 25% of income) for self-employed.

**Short-Term Capital Gain** - Investment profit on asset held ≤1 year, taxed as ordinary income.

**SIMPLE IRA** - Simplified plan for small employers; allows contributions up to $16,000 annual (2024).

**Social Security** - Federal insurance program providing retirement, disability, survivor benefits.

**Solo 401(k)** - Self-employed retirement plan allowing contributions up to $69,000 annual (2024).

**Special Needs Trust** - Trust designed for disabled beneficiary, providing supplements without disqualifying public benefits.

**Spousal IRA** - IRA for non-working spouse, funded by working spouse.

**SSI** - Supplemental Security Income; needs-based government assistance for low-income disabled individuals.

**Standard Deduction** - Tax deduction available to all taxpayers; amount varies by filing status (2024: $13,850 single, $27,700 MFJ).

**Step-Up in Basis** - Receiving inherited asset with basis stepped up to fair market value at death, eliminating income tax on appreciation.

## T

**Tax-Deferred Growth** - Investment growth not taxed until withdrawal (IRAs, 401(k)s, life insurance).

**Tax-Free Growth** - Investment growth not subject to tax (Roth accounts, municipal bond interest).

**Tax Loss Harvesting** - Selling losing positions to offset capital gains, reducing tax liability.

**Testamentary Trust** - Trust created within will, beginning at testator's death.

**Testator** - Person making a will.

**Time Value of Money** - Principle that money today is worth more than same amount in future (due to investment opportunity).

**Trustee** - Person managing trust assets for beneficiaries' benefit.

**UGMA/UTMA** - Uniform Gifts/Transfers to Minors Act; mechanism for transferring assets to minors; student has control at majority.

## U

**Umbrella Liability Insurance** - Additional liability insurance covering above standard homeowners/auto policy limits.

**Unearned Income** - Income from investments (interest, dividends, capital gains); does not include wages.

## V

**Variable Annuity** - Annuity with return depending on underlying investment performance.

## W

**Wash Sale** - Selling security at loss and repurchasing substantially identical security within 30 days (loss disallowed).

**Whole Life Insurance** - Permanent life insurance with cash value, living benefits; premiums level for life.

**Will** - Legal document directing property transfer at death, designating executor and guardians.

## Y

**Yield** - Investment return expressed as percentage; interest rate or dividend yield.
