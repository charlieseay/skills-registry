# Fundamentals of Financial Planning (7th Ed)

Source: Dalton, Dalton, Gillice, Langdon — "Fundamentals of Financial Planning," 7th Edition (924 pages). Extracted for the `financial-advisor-kb` knowledge base. Each chapter below is a standalone note capturing the author's named frameworks, formulas (verbatim), actionable principles, terminology, and anti-patterns — not a narrative summary.

## Chapters

1. **[Introduction to Financial Planning](chapters/01-introduction-to-financial-planning.md)** — the financial planning process, client relationship establishment, data gathering, fiduciary duty, certifications and compensation models.
2. **[Interpersonal Communication, Behavioral Finance & Client Psychology](chapters/02-interpersonal-communication-behavioral-finance.md)** — counseling paradigms, active/reflective listening, cognitive biases, Behavioral Portfolio Theory, money scripts, stages of change.
3. **[Financial Planning Approaches: Analysis and Recommendations](chapters/03-financial-planning-approaches.md)** — the seven core analytical approaches (life cycle, pie chart, statement/ratio analysis, two-step/three-panel/metrics, present value of all goals, cash flow, strategic) with benchmark ratios.
4. **[Personal Financial Statements: Preparation and Analysis](chapters/04-personal-financial-statements.md)** — balance sheet, income statement, statement of net worth, cash flow statement, and every liquidity/debt/performance ratio formula.
5. **[Risk Management for the Individual Client](chapters/05-risk-management.md)** — the risk management process, pure vs. speculative risk, insurance concepts (life, health, disability, property/casualty), needs analysis.
6. **[John and Mary Burke Case and Case Analysis](chapters/06-burke-case-analysis.md)** — reusable case-analysis methodology (data gathering → statement analysis → objective-by-objective recommendation), generalized from the worked case.
7. **[Time Value of Money](chapters/07-time-value-of-money.md)** — every TVM formula (PV/FV/PMT/N/i, annuity due vs. ordinary, serial payments) with sign-convention pitfalls.
8. **[Education and Education Funding](chapters/08-education-funding.md)** — savings vehicles (529, Coverdell, UTMA/UGMA, EE/I bonds), funding calculation methods, financial aid (EFC, FAFSA), tax credits/deductions.
9. **[Investments](chapters/09-investments.md)** — asset classes, modern portfolio theory, CAPM, efficient frontier, risk/return formulas, portfolio construction principles.
10. **[David and Amy Rudolph Case & Case Analysis Part 1](chapters/10-rudolph-case-part1.md)** — reusable case methodology, Part 1 of 2 (data gathering and initial analysis). Continues in Chapter 18.
11. **[Retirement Planning Accumulations and Distributions](chapters/11-retirement-planning-accumulations-distributions.md)** — capital needs analysis models, savings-rate guidance, wage replacement ratio methods, distribution strategies (4% rule, bucket approach). Flagship chapter for retirement-planning queries.
12. **[Income Tax Planning](chapters/12-income-tax-planning.md)** — filing status, AGI/taxable income formula, deductions vs. credits, AMT, basis rules, holding periods.
13. **[Business Entity Selection and Taxation](chapters/13-business-entity-selection-taxation.md)** — entity types compared (sole prop, partnership, S-corp, C-corp, LLC), selection decision framework, self-employment tax.
14. **[Estate Planning](chapters/14-estate-planning.md)** — wills, trusts, powers of attorney, property ownership structures, federal transfer tax framework.
15. **[Economics and the External Environment](chapters/15-economics-external-environment.md)** — business cycle, monetary/fiscal policy, GDP/CPI/inflation formulas, unemployment types, consumer/investor protection (FDIC/SIPC).
16. **[Ethics & Standards of Conduct](chapters/16-ethics-standards-of-conduct.md)** — CFP Board Code of Ethics duties, practice standards, disciplinary framework.
17. **[Planning for Special Circumstances](chapters/17-special-circumstances.md)** — special needs, divorce, terminal illness, non-traditional households, job loss, windfalls.
18. **[David and Amy Rudolph Case & Case Analysis Part 2](chapters/18-rudolph-case-part2.md)** — case resolution: capital needs, asset allocation under concentration, entity selection, estate planning with minors, monitoring. Part 2 of 2; Part 1 is Chapter 10.
